# Study Session Checklist & Mastery Framework

This reusable guide turns each study session into a disciplined ritual that builds permanent mastery. It blends the Copilot-augmented "Mastering Class Notes Forever" phases with the Musashi-inspired principles (Gaku Nomi, Katachi, Shu Chu, Renshu, Mooroku), so training becomes systematic rather than dependent on willpower.

## Reusable Session Checklist
| Stage | Focus | Checklist Items |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pre-Session (Gaku Nomi warm-up)** | Prepare body and mind | - Confirm fixed study block on calendar (e.g., 6–8 PM) and mark attendance. <br> - Gather notes/materials and open Copilot/Notebook workspace. <br> - Review previous Mossoku catalog entry for the unit you are about to practice. <br> - Perform the Katachi ritual: sit properly, breathe three times, state the learning goal aloud.
| **Processing/Encoding (Phase 1 + 2)** | Capture & anchor new material | - Upload/paste raw notes into Copilot and request topic-organized summaries plus highlighted definitions. <br> - Ask Copilot for a concept map outlining the relationships and note the most exam-worthy gaps. <br> - Build or update the Memory Palace for today’s topic: assign 10–15 vivid stations, with motion, humor, and emotional anchors. <br> - Walk the palace immediately, narrating each station aloud to lock imagery.
| **Practice Loop (Shu Chu / Renshu)** | Deliberate retrieval & spacing | - Generate understanding-based flashcards via Copilot (20 Q&A, difficulty tags). <br> - Export into spaced repetition tool (Anki/RemNote/Quizlet) or follow manual Day 1/3/7/14/30 schedule. <br> - Perform active recall: blank-page write-up, Copilot quiz (mixed formats), or teach-back session. <br> - Use Feynman explain-it-like-a-child + ask Copilot to critique and identify jargon gaps. <br> - For tough cards/concepts, request elaborative interrogation prompts (“Why is this true? How does it work?”) before checking model answers.
| **Integration & Mixing (Mooroku maintenance)** | Solidify connections & maintenance | - Interleave topics: Ask Copilot for a mixed-practice quiz sampling multiple subjects, including scenario/application prompts. <br> - Alternate Memory Palace walks between different units (Station 1 from Palace A, then B, etc.). <br> - Catalog mastered items in Mooroku with review dates (Day 1/7/30) and log difficulty ratings/notes. <br> - Review spaced cards due today; mark those needing rephrasing and prompt Copilot for alternative angles.
| **Post-Session Reflection** | Close the loop | - Record what was mastered vs. what still feels fuzzy; adjust next prompt accordingly. <br> - Update study journal with evidence, prompts used, and catalog entries. <br> - Note next session goals (subject/topic) and any new ritual tweaks. <br> - Spend 2–3 minutes walking the palace one last time or explaining the concept out loud to consolidate.

## Mastery Framework & Procedure Manual
1. **Start with Structure (Gaku Nomi + Katachi)**
   - Treat the session start as non-negotiable: fixed time, location, and 3–7 step ritual (posture, breath, goal statement, material read, recall attempt, feedback check).
   - Always state what you want to master before opening the material.
2. **Process Immediately (Phase 1)**
   - Upload notes right after class or reading. Use Copilot prompts such as:
     - "Summarize these notes by topic, each with a definition + real-world example." 
     - "Create a concept map showing how these concepts relate, including cause-and-effect connections."
   - Have Copilot flag likely exam questions and missing related topics; record them in the catalog.
3. **Anchor Spatially (Phase 2)**
   - Build or refresh a Memory Palace for the topic. Use prompts like:
     - "For each concept, suggest a vivid, action-packed mental image suitable for placement in my [chosen location]."
   - Immediately narrate the palace walkthrough to turn imagery into narrative memory.
4. **Practice with Spacing (Phase 3)**
   - Convert the new summary into flashcards rated Easy/Medium/Hard.
   - Export to Anki/RemNote or apply the Day 1/3/7/14/30 manual rhythm.
   - Track mastery status in Mooroku; only mark done once retrieval is perfect and verified.
5. **Force Retrieval (Phases 4–5)**
   - Blank-page recall and Copilot quizzes replicate the forgetting curve; use prompts like:
     - "Generate a 15-question quiz mixing multiple choice, short answer, and true/false; provide the answer key at the end."
     - "Evaluate my simple explanation of [concept]; score clarity/accuracy and suggest analogies."
   - When Copilot exposes errors, stay with the concept (Renshu) until the explanation is spotless.
6. **Go Deeper (Phase 6)**
   - Ask Copilot for "Why is this true?" and "How does it differ from [related concept]?" for each fact.
   - Write your answers before comparing, then log the insights in Mooroku for future review.
7. **Mix & Maintain (Phase 7 + Mooroku)**
   - Interleave questions across topics to build transfer (Prompt: "Create a 20-question mixed practice test shuffling Topics A, B, C with application scenarios").
   - Rotate palace walks between subjects to highlight distinctions.
   - Run scheduled reviews from Mooroku (Day 1/7/30) and adjust difficulty/hints as needed.
8. **Close & Plan Forward**
   - After each session, update the catalog with what was conquered, set reminders for Day 1/7/30 reviews, and note the next topic for Shu Chu focus.
   - Keep an "action prompt log" listing the Copilot prompts that worked best so you can reuse them the next time similar material appears.

## Accessibility & Habit Notes
- Keep written instructions in short paragraphs with clear headings for screen readers.
- Provide alt-text for concept maps or diagrams; describe imagery in the Memory Palace section to ensure non-visual comprehension.
- Maintain consistent navigation order: Pre-session checklist → Processing → Practice → Maintenance → Reflection.
- For each habit, note the keyboard-friendly commands/shortcuts you use (Copilot commands, flashcard shortcuts) so you can replicate them without relying on mouse interactions.
